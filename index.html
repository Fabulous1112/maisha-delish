About us

Serving premium with passion

Premium meals, pastries & drinks

Maisha Delish is a Nigerian food business serving premium cooked meals, savory pastries, sweet treats, cakes and drinks. We cook for families, parties and businesses, and we bring the same care to a single meat pie as to a full event.

Our trays of jollof rice, fried rice, egusi and stews are made fresh to order. Our pastries are freshly baked daily and packed in hygienically sealed packaging.
